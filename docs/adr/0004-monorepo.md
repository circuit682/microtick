# ADR 0004 — Begin Development in a Private Repository

**Status:** Accepted

## Context

The platform is in its validation stage and represents significant
original engineering effort.

---

## Decision

Maintain the repository as private during MVP development.

Future open-source contributions will be evaluated on a
component-by-component basis.

---

## Alternatives Considered

Public repository from inception.

---

## Consequences

The team retains flexibility regarding licensing and commercialization.
